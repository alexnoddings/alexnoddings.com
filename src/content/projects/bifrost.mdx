# Summary

bifröst bridges notifications from [Archipelago.gg](https://archipelago.gg/faq/en/) games to Discord channels.

Progress in AP games is commonly blocked on another player unlocking something for you.
Normally, you only find out you're unblocked by logging in to the game to check, or if a player knows you were waiting for an item and messages you themselves.

bifröst connects to Archipelago games as a fake player, and monitors for unlock messages being sent by the server.
It can then notify users in a Discord channel.
